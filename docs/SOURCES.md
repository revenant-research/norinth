# Sources for factual claims in product copy and docs

Every dated regulatory or market statement that appears on the landing page or
in `docs/` must be listed here with a primary or reputable secondary source.
Anything not listed here must not be asserted as fact.

Statements deliberately **not** made anywhere in product copy, because they are
not sourced: competitors' prices or contract structures, characterizations of
named vendors' products, market-size or survey figures, funding rounds,
hardware sizing numbers, response-time commitments, and any email address
that is not an operator-configured value.
